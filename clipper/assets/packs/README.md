Starter picture packs for Ball Evolution (see `clipper/ball_starters.py`).

Each folder is one pack: `pack.json` (title, hook, names in order, the last
is the final form) and `01.webp`..`08.webp`. The flag packs are countryballs
drawn from the flag-icons SVGs (MIT, `LICENSE-flag-icons`); the crown on
the final one is the Fluent Emoji 3D crown (MIT) already bundled in
`../emoji`.

The logo packs (`logos_*`: phones, apps, cars, gaming, sportswear) are logo
balls -- the brand's colour with its logo in white or black, a crown on the
final one -- drawn from the Simple Icons SVGs (CC0, `LICENSE-simple-icons`).
Simple Icons' data is free, but the logos stay their owners' trademarks:
Dean chose to use them (19 Oct 2026) knowing a brand can file a trademark
complaint; each pack's credit line says the video isn't made or endorsed
by them. `flags_africa` and `flags_asia` were added the same day, drawn
like the first three. The script that drew them ran in the sandbox only
(cairosvg isn't an app dependency). The meme starter packs are plans in `ball_starters.MEME_PLANS`:
their stickers come from GIPHY, which only the running app can reach.
