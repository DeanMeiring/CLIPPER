Flag balls for the Ball Evolution duel and tournament (`clipper/ball_duel.py`,
`COUNTRIES`): flag-icons' square (1x1) SVGs (MIT, `LICENSE-flag-icons`), drawn
to 256 px webp in the sandbox with cairosvg (not an app dependency). Plain
flags, no eyes: the duel draws its own faces on them. Countries in an armed
conflict with each other are left out on purpose.
